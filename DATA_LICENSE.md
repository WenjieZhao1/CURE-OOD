# RADCURE data license and attribution

The sanitized benchmark CSV templates under `data/` are derived from the RADCURE clinical data distributed by The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA). They contain only column headers and patient IDs; clinical and acquisition values are not distributed. The source clinical data are licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The files in this repository contain derived cohort splits for acquisition-aware in-domain and out-of-distribution evaluation. They do not include the corresponding CT images, RTSTRUCT files, or pretrained model weights.

Users must comply with the [TCIA Data Usage Policy and Restrictions](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/data-usage-policies-and-restrictions/) and provide the citation required by TCIA:
